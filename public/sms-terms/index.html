Agreement to these terms

These SMS Text Messaging Terms of Service (these “SMS Terms”) are incorporated into all agreements between you and Thrive Family Counseling, LLC (“our practice,” “us,” “we”), including any agreements that are preexisting and any agreements that might be enacted contemporaneously with these SMS Terms.

What we send by text

Thrive Family Counseling may use SMS text messaging, from time to time, for certain types of communication with you, including administrative matters such as appointment reminders, scheduling, and billing, and for care-related reminders connected to services we are providing to you.

We do not conduct therapy or discuss the substance of clinical care over SMS. If you are experiencing an emergency, call 911 or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — do not rely on text messaging.

Your consent (opt-in)

By providing your mobile number and agreeing to these SMS Terms — on our intake or consent paperwork, verbally with your clinician, or by initiating a text message to our practice number — you agree to receive (you “opt in” to receiving) SMS text messages from Thrive Family Counseling related to services we are providing to you.

  • Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies.
  • Text STOP at any time to opt out of receiving SMS text messages from us.
  • Text HELP at any time to receive help, or call us at (630) 557‑6933.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of receiving services from our practice.

Our numbers

SMS text messages from Thrive Family Counseling may originate from our practice telephone numbers, including our secure practice messaging number provided to you at intake and published on this page once assigned, and our practice line, (630) 557‑6933.

Professional consultation contacts

Where we provide consultation to other professionals or agencies (rather than direct clinical care), text messaging with those professionals is governed by these SMS Terms as well. Clients of those professionals are served under those professionals’ own client agreements, and we do not text-message them under this program.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

SMS opt-in and consent data will not be shared with, sold to, rented to, or disclosed to any third parties. Please see our SMS Privacy Policy for details on how we handle information collected through our text messaging program.

Other agreements

There may be terms in other agreements between you and us that also apply to our practice’s use of SMS text messaging, such as general terms related to privacy and data handling that are not specific to SMS text messaging. You agree that you have reviewed all agreements that we have provided you.
