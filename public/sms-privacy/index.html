SMS Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Effective July 2, 2026

In short: information collected through our text messaging program is used only to provide the messaging you opted in to. No mobile opt-in data or texting consent information is ever shared with or sold to third parties.

Our commitment

Thrive Family Counseling, LLC (“our practice,” “us,” “we”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy describes how we handle information collected as part of our SMS text messaging program. It supplements — and does not replace — our Notice of Privacy Practices and any other privacy terms in agreements between you and our practice.

What we collect

As part of our SMS or text messaging program, we may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your messaging consent and opt-in data (when and how you opted in)
  • The messages exchanged between you and our practice, and delivery metadata

How we use it

Any information collected as part of our SMS or text messaging program, including mobile phone numbers, messaging consent, and opt-in data, is used solely for the purpose of providing the messaging services you have opted in to receive — such as appointment reminders, scheduling, and billing communications.

No third-party sharing

No mobile opt-in data or text messaging consent information will be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to any third parties, except as required to deliver the messaging service (for example, our HIPAA-compliant messaging platform and the wireless carriers that transmit messages) or to comply with applicable laws.

Security and HIPAA

We send text messages through a HIPAA-compliant communications platform operating under a Business Associate Agreement with our practice. Standard SMS is not an encrypted medium once a message reaches your carrier and device, which is one reason we limit texting to administrative content such as scheduling and reminders.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, or by telling your clinician. Reply HELP for assistance, or call us at (630) 557‑6933. Opting out of text messaging does not affect the services you receive from our practice.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or our texting program, contact Thrive Family Counseling at (630) 557‑6933. Our SMS Terms of Service are available at tfcthrive.com/sms-terms.
